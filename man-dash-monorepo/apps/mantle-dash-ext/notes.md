Mantle Dash: Real-time Mantle Network Dashboard

Monitor the Mantle ecosystem instantly from your browser toolbar.

Features:
- **Market:** Live MNT price, market cap, supply, and Bybit K-line chart.
- **Gas:** Real-time gas tracking with high/low fee alerts.
- **Network:** TPS, total txs, block height, and block time.
- **Rollup:** L1 batch submission status.
- **Settings:** Mainnet/Testnet, Dark/Light themes, English/Chinese.

Perfect for developers and traders tracking the pulse of Mantle.
